The panel · 16:20–16:50

From prediction
to intervention.

What changes when mobility systems become agentic?

We plan to bring together perspectives from data mining, recommendation, agents and mobility deployment. Brief opening positions will lead into moderated debate and audience questions.

  • When do agentic capabilities improve decisions?
  • Which assumptions break when people respond?
  • How should we evaluate system-wide consequences?
