Weigang Lu is a Full Professor at the School of Computer Science, Northwest University. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Xidian University in 2025. Prof. Hong Kam Lo

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering

Homepage ↗Email ↗ Hong Kam Lo
Read biography +

Hong Kam Lo is Chair Professor of Civil and Environmental Engineering and Founding Director of the GREAT Smart Cities Institute at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). He received his Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from The Ohio State University in 1991. After working at Oak Ridge National Laboratory and the University of California, Berkeley, he joined HKUST in 1996 and became Associate Professor in 2001, Professor in 2007, and Chair Professor in 2014. His research focuses on intelligent transportation systems, smart cities, and sustainability. He developed Hong Kong’s first agent-based dynamic transportation model, adopted by industry for network service planning, and his research on signalized roundabouts has also been implemented. He works with government and industry partners and serves as founding editor-in-chief of Transportmetrica B: Transport Dynamics and managing editor of the Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems. His awards include the triennial World Conference on Transport Research Award and the School of Engineering Research Excellence Award.
