Call for Papers

Research topics, submission requirements, and review arrangements for AMI’27.

TentativeSubmission details are subject to confirmation.

Scope & Contributions

We welcome methodological, empirical, systems, dataset and position contributions that connect mobility data mining, recommendation, participant interaction and closed-loop learning.

A contribution does not need to implement an entire closed loop. Component-level work is welcome when it explains its implications for downstream decisions or interaction. Well-supported negative results and early-stage contributions are also encouraged.

Topics of interest

01

Mobility data mining and user modeling

  • Geo-location, trajectory, and mobility behavior mining, including user activity and preference modeling.
  • Traffic, demand, travel-time, and mobility forecasting for downstream recommendation and decision making.
  • Large-scale graph and spatiotemporal learning for mobility data.
02

Recommendation and agentic systems for mobility

  • Recommendation, matching, routing, and repositioning for mobility platforms.
  • Agentic recommender systems and intelligent driver, passenger, fleet, delivery, and platform agents.
  • Interactive and agentic mobility assistants for contextual, sequential, and personalized decision making.
03

Interaction, feedback, and closed-loop mobility systems

  • Human–agent and multi-agent interaction, communication, coordination, and competition.
  • Behavioral responses to recommendations and feedback between algorithmic decisions and future mobility states.
  • Closed-loop evaluation through simulation, counterfactual analysis, online experimentation, and real-world deployment.

Across these themes, we encourage attention to location privacy, equitable service access, robustness, user agency and responsible experimentation. Low-level vehicle control, autonomous-driving perception and infrastructure engineering are in scope only when clearly connected to platform-level mining, recommendation or interaction.

Paper Types & Format

6pages max.

Regular papers

Methodological, empirical or systems research with a clear contribution.

4pages max.

Short & other contributions

Short, position, vision, system, dataset or demonstration papers.

References count toward the page limit. Use the ACM sigconf format. We plan to use double-blind review; please prepare an anonymized submission.

Position papers should present a substantive research question and a defensible argument. Systems and dataset papers should explain their scientific value, intended use and evaluation limitations.

Review & Presentation

Review criteria

We plan to obtain at least three reviews for each submission. Criteria include relevance, originality, technical or conceptual soundness, clarity, reproducibility where applicable, and potential to stimulate discussion.

In-person participation

We will require at least one author of each accepted paper to register and present in person. We plan to offer poster presentation opportunities to all accepted contributions, with selected papers also giving talks.

Publication

We plan to include eligible accepted papers in the WSDM Companion Proceedings, provided they are presented in person and meet the final conference requirements. We will confirm the final publication and presentation requirements in the official call.

Responsible research

Discuss relevant data-use constraints and potential privacy, safety or distributional impacts. Make assumptions about feedback and participant behavior explicit.
