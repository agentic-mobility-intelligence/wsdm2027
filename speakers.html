Invited speaker

Prof. Lei Chen

Chair Professor · Dean of Information Hub

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

Prof. Chen works on data management and database systems, including data-driven AI and spatiotemporal data. His expertise connects mobility data analysis with the workshop’s questions about effective real-world decisions.

Invited talk

Title to be announced

Planned focus. The talk is expected to explore how data mining and spatiotemporal intelligence can support reliable, adaptive decisions in dynamic mobility environments. Topics may include decision-making with incomplete information, evolving mobility patterns, and learning from behavioral feedback when interventions influence future observations.

The final title and abstract will be announced.
